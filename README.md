# Hi, I'm Agustí Figueras

**AI Filmmaker · Art Director · Production Designer** — Barcelona, Spain

For 25+ years I've worked in the art department of international commercials and feature films: 600+ commercials, more than 100 of them as art director or production designer, for brands such as Apple, BMW, Ferrari, Adidas and IKEA, and features like *Uncharted* (Sony Pictures).

Now I direct with generative AI. I call it **artisan AI**: the tools are new, the eye is the same. Light, composition, materials and continuity still decide whether an image works, and my standard is that the result should be indistinguishable from a real production.

I run **[Laia Media](https://laiamedia.com)**, an AI-assisted production studio and my R&D lab.

### What I do
- AI films and commercials, from concept to final grade
- Art direction for AI pipelines: look, sets, props, character consistency
- ComfyUI workflows built around a director's needs

**Pipeline:** image generation → video generation → ComfyUI → edit & grade  
**Tools:** ComfyUI (FLUX, SDXL) · Midjourney · Nano Banana · Kling · Runway · Seedance · DaVinci Resolve Studio  
**Now exploring:** LoRA training, ControlNet, Gaussian splatting
